      FAQ

      Good questions

      Are there any hidden fees?

      No. Package prices cover the listed scope plus standard hosting and maintenance. Domain registration, paid third-party services and any additional work are separate, and they're always disclosed up front.

      Do I own my website?

      Ownership, delivery of the site files, and transfer terms are set out in your written agreement before any work begins. Ask and we'll walk you through them for your package.

      Can I update my website?

      Yes. Every monthly plan includes a set amount of minor content edits, like new hours, prices, photos or text changes. Larger changes beyond that allowance are available at $40/hour, and only with your approval first.

      What if I need more pages?

      Bigger projects are welcome. If you need more pages than a package includes, or features like online ordering, you'll get a custom quote before anything starts.

      Can you redesign an existing website?

      Yes. We'll review your current site first, then agree on the scope together before quoting the redesign.

      How long does it take to build a website?

      It depends on how ready your content is (text, photos, logo) and how complex the site is. You'll get a timeline for your project before work begins.

      Can I cancel monthly management?

      Ongoing management plans and the 12-month subscription work differently, and the cancellation terms for each are spelled out in your written agreement before you sign. Ask anytime if you'd like to see them first.
